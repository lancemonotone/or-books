## 1. Project Description

Developer will provide website development and related consulting services for Client's existing BigCommerce storefront at orbooks.com (and Client's BigCommerce staging storefront as needed). Work focuses on the custom Stencil theme, storefront presentation, and agreed technical improvements so the site remains maintainable and consistent with Client's brand and publishing needs.

Work is tracked and scoped through written task briefs (including Briefboard or email), with estimates provided upon request.

## 2. Scope of Work

Developer will perform mutually agreed development and consulting work, which may include:

- Custom BigCommerce Stencil theme development and refinement (templates, styles, scripts).
- Design and UX fixes across storefront templates (product, category, CMS, listing, and related pages).
- Staging storefront setup, theme push/deploy assistance, and related BigCommerce configuration support within Developer's technical scope.
- Investigation and remediation of storefront bugs or regressions caused by prior theme work, where agreed in writing.
- Brief written notes or staging review support tied to completed tasks, when included in the agreed scope for that task.

All work under this Agreement is billed hourly as described in Section 6.

## 3. Exclusions

The following are not included unless separately agreed in writing:

- Building a new website or migrating off BigCommerce.
- BigCommerce platform subscription, apps/extensions Client installs, payment gateways, shipping carriers, tax services, or other third-party store services.
- Ongoing catalog management as a primary service (bulk product data entry, merchandising strategy, or day-to-day product uploads), beyond limited technical assistance expressly agreed per task.
- Ongoing content creation, copywriting, photography, or marketing strategy.
- Legal advice (including font licensing or intellectual-property disputes with third parties).
- Guarantees regarding third-party enforcement actions, hosting uptime, or BigCommerce platform changes outside Developer's control.

Any such work may be added later under a separate written agreement or as Additional Services.

## 4. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

- Provide branding assets, content, credentials (as needed for staging/production work), and decisions required to complete agreed tasks.
- Ensure Client holds any required licenses for fonts, images, and other third-party assets used on the storefront (Developer does not supply or warrant Client's font or media licenses).
- Review work on staging (or other agreed preview), provide feedback, and approve deliverables in a timely manner.
- Designate one primary point of contact for approvals and decisions (Colin Robinson, unless Client notifies Developer otherwise in writing).

Delays in providing content, access, or approvals may extend timelines and may result in additional billable time if Developer must rework or re-deploy because of late changes.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Costs

Client is responsible for all third-party services and fees, including but not limited to:

- BigCommerce plan and channel fees.
- Domain name registration and DNS.
- SSL (typically included with BigCommerce).
- Email, newsletter, analytics, CDN, or other tools Client uses with the storefront.
- Font licenses, stock media, and app/extension subscriptions.

Developer may recommend providers or approaches and assist with technical setup as agreed. Client will contract with and pay these providers directly. These costs are not included in Developer's fees.

## 6. Fees, Payment, and Timeline

**Hourly rate:** 125 USD per hour.

Work under this Agreement is billed hourly against mutually agreed tasks and estimates. Estimates are good-faith projections, not fixed quotes, unless Developer explicitly states a fixed fee for a defined deliverable in writing.

**Invoicing:** Developer will invoice Client periodically. Each invoice will state a due date (typically 15 days from the invoice date). Invoices are due on or before the due date. Payment must be received by Developer on or before that date. Payment via Zelle to lancemonotone@gmail.com is preferred; check is also acceptable (Client is responsible for mailing time so that payment arrives on or before the due date).

**Past-due payments:** Developer may pause work, withhold theme pushes to staging or production, and/or withhold deliverables if payments are past due.

**Timeline:** Individual tasks will include estimated effort and target dates where practical. Overall schedule depends on timely Client feedback, access, and payment. Timelines are estimates and may be adjusted by mutual agreement if delays occur outside Developer's control.

## 7. Quarterly Maintenance (Optional)

Beginning when Client opts in in writing (email is sufficient), Developer will provide Quarterly Maintenance every three (3) months, unless either party terminates these services in writing. Unless Client opts in, there is no automatic maintenance retainer under this Agreement.

**Quarterly Maintenance Fee:** 300 USD per quarter, covering approximately two (2) hours of work per cycle.

Each Quarterly Maintenance cycle includes:

- Reviewing the storefront/theme environment as needed (where Client's BigCommerce access allows).
- Applying agreed theme updates and related checks for basic functionality and security.
- Assisting with routine BigCommerce staging/production theme publishes for maintenance releases.
- Checking for issues and addressing problems that arise directly from these maintenance updates.
- Simple or minor content updates (such as updating text, icons, or images) within the approximate two-hour allowance.
- General availability during the cycle to answer questions and help Client strategize how best to use the site.

Client will be invoiced 300 USD at the beginning of each maintenance period; payment must be received on or before the due date (same terms as Section 6).

**Out of scope:** Development, major technical additions, redesigns, catalog operations, or features requiring programming outside the routine scope above are billed separately at 125 USD per hour under Section 6 / Section 8. If such work is performed during a scheduled maintenance cycle, the 300 USD Quarterly Maintenance Fee for that cycle is credited toward the development charges for that work.

## 8. Additional Services

Work outside an approved task brief, or beyond any separately agreed maintenance arrangement, will be treated as Additional Services. This includes, for example:

- New features, templates, or pages not in the prior brief.
- Major design changes after Client approval of a direction or deliverable.
- Consulting beyond short handover/training for a completed task.
- Significant content entry, data migration, or catalog operations not described in the approved brief.
- Emergency or rush work outside normal scheduling, if Developer accepts it.

Additional Services are billed at the hourly rate in Section 6 (125 USD per hour), with Client's prior approval (email approval is sufficient). Developer may provide estimates upon request; estimates are not fixed quotes unless explicitly stated as such.

Developer will provide reasonable training or written notes (as scoped per task) on how to review or operate relevant storefront changes. Ongoing support beyond this period is provided through Quarterly Maintenance (Section 7) and/or Additional Services (Section 8). Developer cannot guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation due to hosting, internet, BigCommerce platform, or other third-party service issues.

## 12. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party as provided herein, or until both parties agree in writing that the engagement is complete.

Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches its obligations and does not cure the breach within 15 days of written notice.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 15 days' written notice.

If Client terminates for convenience (i.e., without Developer breach):
